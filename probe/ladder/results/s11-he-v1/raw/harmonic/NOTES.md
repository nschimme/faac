# Missing-harmonic SBR probe

This worktree starts at upstream `99ce2b0980180194f9ccd1993a786208d277cf3e` and applies `.cache/he-v1-feature-study/invf/invf-probe.patch` as diagnostic infrastructure. The missing-harmonic path is opt-in through `FAAC_SBR_MISSING_TONE` / `FAAC_SBR_MISSING_RATIO` or `FAAC_SBR_MISSING_HARMONIC=1`; with all `FAAC_SBR_*` variables unset, the additional analysis and syntax remain inactive.

For each high-resolution envelope band, the detector chooses the QMF bin with maximum existing per-bin tonality. It compares that value with tonality at `sourceMap[k]`, and checks concentration as the energy at that same QMF bin divided by summed band energy, using the same envelope/time support. Neighboring-band detections whose strongest bins differ by one are reduced to the stronger candidate. Per-band state retains detections with half the normal tone and ratio thresholds on the next frame. The SBR add-harmonic flag and one bit per high-resolution band travel through the delayed frame FIFO; bit writing uses `PutBit`.

FAAD3 injects an added sine at the center of the flagged band. The detector selects a strongest bin rather than estimating the decoder's exact center frequency, so the implementation traces `maxMidOffset` and is exploratory. The `missing_midpoint` runner variant adds an exploratory requirement that the selected bin lie within one QMF bin of the decoder insertion midpoint. The optional `FAAC_SBR_HARMONIC_MAX_ENERGY=1` changes flagged-band envelope estimation from average band power to the maximum mean-QMF-bin energy times band width; it can increase envelope levels and bitrate. Neither behavior is a standards-calibrated detector.

`probe/run_harmonic.py` accepts `--clips gate|all`, comma-separated `--variants`, `--rates`, `--jobs`, and `--output-dir`. It forces HE-AAC v1 through the benchmark encoder adapter, requires the HE-AAC ffprobe profile, uses strict FDK decode, scores with Zimtohrli, and computes BD-rate with the benchmark helper. It flushes completed rows atomically. The initial synthetic trace on the prior concentration implementation detected 96 tone frames versus 2 white-noise frames; the detector was then corrected to use same-bin energy concentration, so those counts are not evidence for the corrected implementation. The base tonality estimator is finite and caps near-perfect tones at about 999999 by design. Scoring may begin after the parent reviews the corrected source and rerun controls/synthetics.

Release build: `meson setup build-probe --buildtype=release`, then `CCACHE_DISABLE=1 ninja -C build-probe` (the managed sandbox cannot write to the configured user ccache directory).

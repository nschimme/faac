## Named-family coverage

The bounded study has recorded measured gates/full runs and dispositions for each named arm:

- **Noise18 + limiter + tier17:** retained upstream baseline context; tier17/noise/limiter lineage and controls are archived. The merged baseline is upstream PR #47, not a new S11 change.
- **Adaptive noise; inverse filtering/joint noise; frequency-local noise/inverse filtering:** screened and full-corpus comparisons are archived; the measured candidates regressed or did not establish a matched gain. Inverse-filter/joint family covered six full variants and 1,176 variant rows; frequency-local covered four variants, 1,176 rows and 1,568 strict decoder streams.
- **Harmonic detector:** five detector conditions, 980 rows, controls and 1,764 strict decoder streams; no resolved tone/ratio/midpoint gain, with max-energy ablation regressing.
- **Grid/envelope frequency resolution and frequency tables:** four grid variants, 784 feature rows, controls and 1,176 strict streams; late FIXVAR regressed and remaining policies were inconclusive. Five table settings had 16 unset controls and 112 strict streams without a screening gain.
- **Envelope count/tier17:** 49-clip/four-rate comparisons, fixed-count attribution, validation, timing and footprint retained; the tier17 hybrid merged in PR #47. The unconstrained adaptive selector did not resolve gain over fixed4. Historical timing/state costs remain explicitly tied to their original candidate context.
- **Cross-frame policy:** mode2/future-pcm0/λ=.003 full and gate evidence with direct tier17 comparisons; it did not resolve an advantage over simpler tier17 and was not retained.
- **Transient border:** historical mode1 evidence is kept separate from fresh merged-098 fixed8/adaptive studies. The fresh full matrices, strict decoder gates and distinct PCM equivalence scopes are recorded above.

The archive also preserves source patches and raw result families so that rejected arms remain auditable rather than silently disappearing. Historical stages are cross-referenced in the S11 ledger; S11 extends the S6-N local-noise screen, does not reopen S10's dead long-block arms, and does not claim Apple/FDK parity from baseline-relative BD-rate.
